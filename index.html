BEFORE YOU CALL

A few helpful answers.

Is this a law firm?

No. This is an independent referral and advertising resource. We do not employ an attorney to represent you, offer legal advice, or decide whether you have a claim.

Who will answer my call?

Once an active provider is arranged, calls may be answered by a participating provider or an intake service. Ask who is answering and which attorney or firm would review your inquiry. The current number is a placeholder; no live connection is available.

Does calling cost anything?

Your telephone provider’s normal charges may apply. Ask the receiving provider about any consultation fees, attorney fees, and expenses before agreeing to services. No specific fee arrangement is promised by this site.

Do you cover my part of the Valley?

This resource is intended for the entire San Fernando Valley in the Los Angeles area. A provider’s actual coverage and ability to accept a case must be confirmed directly.

What should I have ready?

If available, have the incident date and location, a brief description, and basic insurance or report information. You do not need every document to ask an initial question. Avoid sharing sensitive details until you know who is receiving them.

Am I required to hire someone?

No. You decide whether to contact or hire an attorney. A call does not guarantee representation or create an attorney-client relationship.

Where can I find another option?

You can use the State Bar of California’s resources to locate a certified lawyer referral service and verify an attorney’s license. See the independent resources linked below.
